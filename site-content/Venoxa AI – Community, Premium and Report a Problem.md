## Community

The Venoxa community is a place to practice together, ask questions, and celebrate progress. Everyone is at a different level, so be patient and kind.

### Community guidelines

- **Be respectful.** No harassment, hate speech, or discrimination.
- **Help, don't mock.** Mistakes are how people learn.
- **Stay on topic.** Keep posts about language learning.
- **Protect privacy.** Don't share personal details of yourself or others.
- **No spam or ads,** and no copied content you don't have the right to share.
- **Keep it safe.** No adult, violent, or illegal content.

Posts that break these rules may be removed, and repeated violations can lead to suspension under our Terms of Service. See something wrong? Use "Report a problem".

## Report a Problem

Help us improve Venoxa. Tell us what went wrong and we will look into it.

### What to include

1. **Type of problem:** bug, wrong lesson or AI answer, payment issue, or abusive content or user.
2. **What happened** and what you expected to happen.
3. **Where:** the page or lesson, and the time it happened.
4. **Your device and browser,** for example Chrome on Android.
5. **A screenshot,** if you can.

### How to send it

Use the report form at \[form link\], or email \[support email\] with the subject "Problem report". Do not include your password or payment card details.
