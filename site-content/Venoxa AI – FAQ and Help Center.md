## FAQ

**What is Venoxa AI?** A language learning web app that combines lessons, exercises, and an AI tutor for practice and instant feedback.

**Which languages can I learn?** Venoxa is available in English and teaches \[list languages\]. We add new courses regularly.

**Is Venoxa free?** Yes, you can start for free. Premium unlocks extra features such as \[list features\]. See the Premium page for details.

**Do I need to download anything?** No. Venoxa runs in your browser on desktop and mobile.

**How does the AI feedback work?** When you write or speak, the AI checks your answer, explains mistakes, and suggests improvements. It can occasionally be wrong, so use it as a guide and double-check important points.

**Is my progress saved across devices?** Yes. Log in with the same account and your progress syncs automatically.

**How do I reset my password?** Select "Forgot password" on the login page and follow the email link.

**How do I cancel Premium or delete my account?** Both are in your account settings. Cancelling keeps Premium until the end of the period you paid for.

**Is my data safe?** We protect it with encryption and never sell it. Read our Privacy Policy for details.

**I found a mistake or a bug. What do I do?** Use "Report a problem" in the footer and tell us what happened.

## Help Center

### Getting started

1. Create an account with your email.
2. Choose the language you want to learn and take the short placement quiz.
3. Set a daily goal and start your first lesson.
4. Practice with the AI tutor to build speaking and writing confidence.

### Your account

- **Change email or password:** Settings > Account.
- **Change language or level:** Settings > Learning.
- **Delete your account:** Settings > Account > Delete account. This permanently removes your data.

### Billing and Premium

- **Upgrade:** open the Premium page and choose a plan.
- **Cancel:** Settings > Subscription. You keep access until the period ends.
- **Refunds:** see section 8 of the Terms of Service, or email \[support email\].

### Troubleshooting

| Problem | Try this |
| --- | --- |
| Can't log in | Reset your password, then check your email's spam folder for the reset link |
| Progress not syncing | Refresh the page, log out and back in, and make sure you use the same account |
| Lesson or audio won't load | Check your connection, disable extensions that block content, or try another browser |
| AI feedback looks wrong | Report it via "Report a problem" with a screenshot |

Still stuck? Contact us at \[support email\].
